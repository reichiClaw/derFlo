Private Cooking

Four formats.
One chef. Your evening.

A long menu or a relaxed buffet: what ends up on your table depends on your occasion, your guests and your wishes. The four formats are the starting point – everything else we work out together.

  1. 01

    Fine Dining

    A menu that exists for this one evening only.

    An individually composed menu in several courses, plated right at your place. For evenings where the food is allowed to take centre stage – and still nobody has to leave the table to disappear into the kitchen.

    Good for: birthdays, anniversaries, small parties with time on their hands.

  2. 02

    Shared Table

    Everything in the middle. Then everyone digs in.

    Large platters, shared bowls, one table where food is passed around. Less ceremony, more conversation – and a very direct relationship with what you eat.

    Good for: groups of friends, families, relaxed evenings in the chalet.

  3. 03

    Flying Buffet

    Small dishes that come to your guests.

    Instead of a laid table, there are many small courses that make their way through the room one after another. Your guests stay on their feet, get talking – and are looked after really well along the way.

    Good for: receptions, celebrations with more guests, occasions without a seating plan.

  4. 04

    Traditional Cuisine

    Familiar dishes, made with care.

    Dishes you know and love – held to the same standard I apply in the restaurant: good ingredients, clean craft, no unnecessary frills. Down to earth, but never arbitrary.

    Good for: family gatherings and evenings with guests who simply want to eat well.

Good to know

Shopping, tableware, service staff, drinks, clearing up: what I take care of and what you organise yourself is something we settle in advance – depending on the occasion, location and number of guests. That way there are no surprises on the evening, just good food.

About Flo

Hello, I’m Flo.

A chef by conviction, head chef at restaurant KITSCH in Zürs am Arlberg – and, as derFlo, your personal chef for evenings that take place at your home.

At KITSCH I cook night after night for guests who appreciate good food. Our style there sits somewhere between alpine-regional, world cuisine and French classics – modern, but uncomplicated. My career also includes Villa Joya in Portugal.

For me, private cooking is the most personal form of cooking: I stand in your kitchen, you sit with your guests. I see how the food lands at the table, and you don’t have to worry about anything except good company.

No catering company, no standard menus. I’m the one at the stove – and we talk to each other directly beforehand, not through a booking engine.

How it works

Three steps to your evening.

You don’t need to know exactly what you want. It’s enough to know what you feel like.

  1. 1

    You tell me about your occasion.

    When, where, how many guests, what kind of mood. By email, phone or via the enquiry form below. Rough details are perfectly fine to start with.

  2. 2

    We agree on the menu and the setting.

    Format, courses, preferences, intolerances, the kitchen on site, tableware, service, drinks: we go through everything together until it fits. You receive a concrete proposal.

  3. 3

    I cook. You host.

    On the evening itself I take care of the food. You take care of your guests – or of nothing at all.

Where?

Lech am Arlberg and the surrounding area is home – in the chalet, in the holiday apartment, at your place. For other locations, simply ask, and we’ll see together what can be arranged.
